### New Active Decisions

#### 1. Three-Service Boundary
- **By:** Daisy (Lead)
- **What:** CloudExpense Lite uses exactly three services: expense-api, workflow-engine, notification-svc.
- **Why:** Minimum viable distributed story to show Dapr service invocation, workflows, pub/sub, and state without bloat.

#### 2. Dapr Workflows as Orchestration Model
- **By:** Daisy (Lead)
- **What:** Use Dapr Workflows (not Durable Functions) for expense approval flow.
- **Why:** Cloud-agnostic, demonstrates checkpoint state, portable across clouds.

#### 3. Azure Container Apps over AKS
- **By:** Daisy (Lead)
- **What:** Deploy to ACA, not AKS.
- **Why:** Managed Dapr sidecar injection, faster provisioning, no Kubernetes expertise required.

#### 4. Explicit Shared Contracts
- **By:** Daisy (Lead)
- **What:** All event and request shapes live in `CloudExpense.Contracts` namespace (C# records).
- **Why:** Prevents drift, makes demo traceable, enables parallel work without merge conflicts.

#### 5. Threshold: Auto-Approve Only When Amount < $100.00
- **By:** Daisy (Lead), Confirmed by Karen
- **What:** Expenses under $100.00 auto-approve; $100.00 and above enter manual review (not auto-rejected).
- **Why:** Clear distinction between terminal rejection and hold-for-review preserves demo credibility.

#### 6. Tracing Model: ExpenseId + CorrelationId
- **By:** Daisy (Lead), Enforced by Karen
- **What:** ExpenseId is the stable business identifier; CorrelationId is the end-to-end tracing identifier created at submission and reused by decision and notification contracts.
- **Why:** Allows clean tracing from submission → decision → notification without multiplying IDs.

#### 7. UTC Timestamp Suffixes for All Public Timestamps
- **By:** Daisy (Lead), Enforced by Karen
- **What:** All datetime fields in contracts are explicitly named with `...Utc` suffix.
- **Why:** Eliminates timezone ambiguity in demo and future serialization tests.

#### 8. Distinguish ManualReviewRequested from ExpenseRejected
- **By:** Daisy (Lead), Enforced by Karen
- **What:** Amounts >= $100.00 trigger `ManualReviewRequested` event, not rejection.
- **Why:** Preserves the distinction between hold and terminal denial, enabling future multi-tier approval without schema rewrite.

#### 9. Dapr App IDs Standardized on Service Folder Names
- **By:** Billy (Backend Dev)
- **What:** Dapr app IDs are `expense-api`, `workflow-engine`, `notification-svc`.
- **Why:** Graham can wire Radius without reverse-engineering app names.

#### 10. Phase Gating: Phase 1 Must Complete Before Phase 2
- **By:** Daisy (Lead)
- **What:** Parallel work (Billy + Graham) authorized only after Phase 1 scaffold passes validation gate.
- **Why:** Prevents merge conflicts, ensures contract stability before implementation diverges.

#### 11. Validation Is Evidence-Based, Not Test-Driven (Phase 1)
- **By:** Karen (Tester)
- **What:** Phase 1 validation uses checklist + fresh build/parse evidence, not a new test harness.
- **Why:** Repo started greenfield; adding a test framework would invent a stack instead of validating the one the team is about to build.

## Key Decisions & Rationale

### Why Evidence-Based Validation?
The repo started greenfield. Adding a test harness at Phase 1 would invent a testing stack instead of validating the one the team is about to build. Karen's checklist + fresh build/parse evidence proved sufficient and kept the gate focused on structural integrity.

### Why ManualReviewRequested Instead of Rejection?
Terminal denials (fraud, compliance) are rejection. Expenses needing human review (> $100) are a hold. Separating these in Phase 1 contracts prevents a Phase 2+ rewrite when the workflow gains multi-tier approval.

### Why CorrelationId + ExpenseId Instead of One ID?
ExpenseId is the business identifier (stable across systems). CorrelationId is the tracing glue (created at submission, reused by all downstream decisions and notifications). Two IDs are the minimum necessary; adding more would muddy the demo story.

### Why UTC Timestamp Suffixes?
Time zones are a hidden source of demo confusion. Explicitly naming fields as `...Utc` prevents assumptions about serialization and future timezone handling.
